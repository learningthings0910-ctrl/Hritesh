BIRTHDAY SURPRISE WEBSITE — REPLACEABLE ASSETS

Open index.html in Chrome or Edge. Keep the folder structure intact.

REPLACE THE 12 MEMORY PHOTOS
Open assets/memories/. Replace the placeholder image files with your real photos, keeping the exact same filenames:
photo-01.jpg
photo-02.jpg
...
photo-12.jpg

For easiest replacement, use JPG images and keep the filenames unchanged. The captions are already attached to the corresponding photo numbers in the website. No upload controls are shown on the site.

REPLACE THE VIDEO
Replace assets/gift-video.mp4 with your video, keeping that exact filename. For best compatibility, use an MP4 encoded with H.264 video and AAC audio. The video page will play it directly; there is no upload control on the page.
You can also replace assets/gift-video-poster.jpg with a still image/poster for the video.

OTHER FILES
- index.html: main webpage shell
- style.css: colours, layout, and styling
- script.js: page navigation, captions, and birthday letter
- assets/bg-*.jpeg: background images already used by the original site

Important: replace the existing placeholder files inside the extracted website folder; don't put the new photos in a separate folder unless you also change the paths in script.js.
